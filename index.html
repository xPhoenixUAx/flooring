Using the Platform

How does the request process work?

Share the basic details of your flooring project. The platform organizes the request and may introduce it to independent providers. Providers decide whether to respond and determine their own availability, recommendations, estimates, scope, and terms.

Share details Request organized Provider conversations
Does submitting a request guarantee a provider response?

No. A request may be shared with independent providers who may fit the project details, but provider availability and interest vary. The platform does not guarantee a response, match, estimate, or project outcome.

Is there a cost to submit a flooring request?

The request form does not display a submission fee. Any project pricing, consultation charges, deposits, or other terms must be confirmed directly with the responding provider before you proceed.

Providers & Contact

Who determines the project scope and recommendations?

Each independent provider determines their own recommendations, proposed scope, estimate, availability, and terms after reviewing the information and any property-specific conditions they consider relevant.

Can I compare information from more than one provider?

If more than one provider responds, you may review and compare the information they provide. Consider scope, preparation, materials, exclusions, timing, and terms—not price alone.

Does the platform verify licenses or insurance?

Requirements and verification practices vary by location and project type. Ask each provider directly for current license, insurance, registration, or other documentation that may apply before agreeing to work.

Project Details

What details should I prepare before submitting?

Helpful starting details include the room or area, approximate project size, current surface, material direction, visible concerns, desired timing, property location, and any access considerations. You do not need to diagnose the floor yourself.

Do I need to know the exact flooring material?

No. You can describe the appearance, performance needs, room, or materials you are considering. A provider can discuss product-specific options and requirements directly with you.

Can I request repair or refinishing instead of replacement?

Yes. Describe the existing floor, visible wear or damage, and the result you are considering. A provider can evaluate whether repair, refinishing, partial replacement, or another approach is appropriate for the property.

Should I include photos or measurements?

If a provider requests them later, photos and approximate measurements may help explain the project. They do not replace an on-site review. Do not upload sensitive documents or images containing personal information that is not needed for the project.

Estimates & Terms

Who provides the estimate?

Independent providers create and deliver their own estimates. The platform does not prepare, approve, verify, or set estimate amounts or contract terms.

What should I review in an estimate?

Review the proposed scope, preparation, materials, quantities, transitions, removal or disposal, timing assumptions, exclusions, payment terms, and how changes would be handled. Ask the provider to explain anything unclear.

When am I committed to a project?

Submitting a request through the platform does not itself create a flooring contract. Review the provider’s documents and terms carefully and confirm directly with the provider when and how an agreement becomes binding. This is general information, not legal advice.
