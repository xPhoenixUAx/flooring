Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy explains how the operator of Flooring Match collects, uses, discloses, stores, and otherwise handles information when you browse this website, contact us, or submit a flooring project request.

At a glance

Flooring Match is an introduction and project-matching resource. It does not perform flooring work, inspect properties, prepare provider estimates, or enter into the service contract between a homeowner and an independent provider.

  • We collect the information needed to understand and route a project request.
  • Relevant request details may be shared with independent providers who may be suitable for the project.
  • The current site does not request payment-card information or use advertising or analytics tracking.

1. Scope and our role

This policy applies to information handled through the Flooring Match website and its project-request process. It does not govern an independent flooring provider's website, communications, inspection process, contract, payment system, or other business practices.

The business operating Flooring Match is responsible for the information it controls through this website. Operator identity, business address, and a dedicated privacy contact must be completed in the site configuration before live production submissions are accepted.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

The project request form may collect:

  • your name and email address;
  • the flooring service or project direction you select;
  • project details you choose to include, such as the rooms involved, current surface, material interests, timing, or visible concerns;
  • your acknowledgement or consent relating to the request and its potential sharing with independent providers; and
  • information included in later messages you send to us.

The current form does not ask for a telephone number, payment-card details, photographs, government identification, or a precise street address. Please do not include sensitive personal information in the free-text project description unless it is genuinely necessary.

Information generated when the site is used

Like most hosted websites, the server and security infrastructure may process technical information needed to deliver and protect the service. This may include an IP address, browser and device information, referring page, requested URL, date and time, response status, and security or error logs. This information is used for operation, troubleshooting, abuse prevention, and service integrity.

Preference information

When you make a privacy choice, the site stores the value flooring-match-cookie-choice in local storage on your browser. It remembers whether the site has already shown you the privacy notice. Details appear in the Cookie Policy.

3. How we use information

We may use information for the following purposes:

  • to receive, organize, review, and respond to a project request;
  • to identify independent flooring providers who may serve the relevant project type or area;
  • to introduce the request to a potential provider and facilitate follow-up communications;
  • to administer consent and site preferences;
  • to maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the website and request process;
  • to prevent fraud, spam, misuse, or unauthorized access;
  • to keep appropriate business and compliance records; and
  • to comply with legal obligations and protect rights, safety, and property.

4. Legal bases for processing

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, processing may rely on one or more of the following:

  • Your request or pre-contractual steps: to take the steps you ask us to take when you submit a project request.
  • Consent: where you agree to a particular use or disclosure and may withdraw that consent for future processing.
  • Legitimate interests: to operate and secure the matching service, communicate about requests, improve workflows, and prevent misuse, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Legal obligations: to retain or disclose information when required by law.

The basis that applies depends on the context, location, and purpose of the processing.

5. How we disclose information

We do not describe project-request information as being sold. We may disclose information to:

  • Independent flooring providers when an introduction appears relevant to the request. Information shared may include your contact details and the project details needed to understand the opportunity.
  • Service providers that support hosting, email delivery, security, maintenance, or other operational functions. They should receive only the information reasonably needed to perform their role.
  • Professional advisers and authorities when reasonably necessary to obtain advice, comply with law, respond to valid legal process, prevent harm, or protect legal rights.
  • A successor organization in connection with a merger, financing, reorganization, sale of assets, or similar business transaction, subject to appropriate safeguards.

6. Independent providers

Independent providers are separate businesses, not employees or agents of Flooring Match. Once a provider receives information and communicates with you, it may process that information for its own purposes, including evaluating the project, scheduling a visit, preparing an estimate, or administering a service relationship.

Providers are responsible for their own privacy notices, security, records, and legal obligations. You should review a provider's practices before sending additional personal, property, financial, or contractual information directly to it.

7. International processing

Website, email, security, or infrastructure vendors may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, the operator should use recognized transfer mechanisms and contractual or organizational safeguards. The countries and mechanisms depend on the production vendors selected for the service.

8. Retention

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including request administration, provider introductions, security, dispute management, recordkeeping, and legal compliance.

Retention periods may differ by category. Relevant factors include whether a request remains active, whether communications or a dispute are ongoing, whether a legal retention period applies, and whether the information is needed to establish or defend legal claims. Information may be deleted or anonymized when it is no longer required.

9. Security

Reasonable administrative and technical measures should be used to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. The current form also uses basic validation, a hidden anti-spam field, and same-origin request checks.

No internet transmission, email route, or storage system is completely secure. Please avoid sending unnecessary confidential information in a project description and contact the operator promptly if you believe information has been mishandled.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live and subject to applicable exceptions, you may have the right to:

  • request access to personal information and information about how it is used;
  • ask for inaccurate or incomplete information to be corrected;
  • request deletion or restriction of processing;
  • object to processing based on certain legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent for future processing where consent is the basis;
  • receive certain information in a portable format; and
  • submit a complaint to a competent data-protection or privacy authority.

We may need to verify your identity and clarify the scope of a request. Exercising a right will not normally affect the lawfulness of processing completed before the request. Some rights are limited by legal exemptions or retention obligations.

11. Children's privacy

The service is intended for adults arranging property-related flooring work. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly seek personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information, contact the operator so it can be reviewed and deleted where appropriate.

12. Third-party sites and services

The website may link to independent providers or other third-party services. Their websites and communications are governed by their own terms and privacy practices. Flooring Match is not responsible for third-party privacy or security practices.

13. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the request form, provider-routing process, storage technologies, vendors, business model, or legal requirements change. Material changes should be communicated in an appropriate manner, and the “Last updated” date identifies the current version.

14. Contact and privacy requests

Questions, privacy requests, and complaints may be submitted through the website contact route. A dedicated privacy email, the legal name of the operator, and its business address must be published here before the service accepts live production submissions.

Production note: This policy is written to match the current website implementation. It should receive jurisdiction-specific legal review after the operating entity, service region, vendors, retention schedule, and provider-routing process are finalized.
