02 / Project Situations

Different starting points. One place to describe the work.

Installation and replacement projects can begin with different rooms, surfaces, and levels of certainty.

You do not need to identify every technical requirement before submitting a request.

  1. A new surface

    Planning flooring for a room, addition, or newly finished space.

  2. Full replacement

    Removing an existing surface and considering a new flooring direction.

  3. Connected rooms

    Coordinating materials, transitions, and layout across more than one area.

03 / What Shapes the Project

A few practical details can shape the installation conversation.

Rooms, existing surfaces, material direction, and access can all influence what a provider may need to review.

You can share approximate information. A provider confirms project-specific requirements.

  1. The space

    Room use, approximate size, and connections to nearby areas provide a useful starting point.

    Share: room or area + approximate size

  2. What exists

    The current surface may affect removal, preparation, height, and transition discussions.

    Share: current material + visible concerns

  3. Material direction

    A preferred material helps focus the conversation, but “not sure” is also a valid answer.

    Share: material, look, or performance priorities

  4. Access & timing

    Furniture, occupied rooms, entry points, and desired timing may shape practical planning.

    Share: access notes + general timing

04 / From Request to Conversation

A simple request. A more useful conversation.

Share the project details you know. The platform may introduce your request to independent providers who decide whether to respond.

No guaranteed response, estimate, recommendation, or project outcome.

How the Request Moves

  1. Share the project

    Describe the rooms, current surface, project direction, ZIP code, and general timing.

  2. Request is organized

    Your details create a clearer starting brief for possible provider conversations.

  3. Continue with providers

    Providers who respond discuss the property, recommendations, estimates, scope, and terms directly with you.

Questions Worth Discussing

  1. What preparation or removal is included?

  2. Which material, product, and installation direction are proposed?

  3. How will transitions, access, and occupied rooms be handled?

  4. What is included, excluded, or assumed in the estimate?

Ask providers to explain anything that is unclear or specific to your property.

05 / Before You Move Forward

Review the full proposal, not only the finished surface.

A useful conversation should make the proposed preparation, materials, timing, and terms easier to understand.

Project details vary by property and provider. Ask for clarification before agreeing to work.

  1. Preparation

    What removal, surface preparation, leveling, or underlayment is included?

  2. Materials

    Which product, finish, quantity, and installation direction are proposed?

  3. Transitions

    How will doorways, adjoining floors, trim, and finished heights be handled?

  4. Access

    What needs to be moved, cleared, protected, or unavailable during the work?

  5. Timing

    What sequence and timing assumptions are included in the proposal?

  6. Estimate & terms

    What is included, excluded, allowed for, and subject to change?
